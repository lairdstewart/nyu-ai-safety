Getting Started

Resources for students who want to learn more about, or get involved in, AI safety.

  • A personal letter on transformative AI. A short case for why transformative AI matters.
  • The Future of AI, BlueDot Impact. Free, self-paced intro course with no prerequisites.
  • Robert Miles. Approachable YouTube explainers on core AI safety problems.
  • ARENA. Self-study curriculum for the ML engineering skills used in AI safety research.
  • Research fellowships: MATS, LASR Labs, Anthropic Fellows, and SPAR.
  • Job and fellowship boards: aisopportunities.com and jobs.80000hours.org.
  • Events in NYC: our Calendar and the Bulletin Board.
  • Collider. Cowork and collaboration space for the AI safety community in NYC.
